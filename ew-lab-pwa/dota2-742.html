Gameplay Update · November 18, 2026

Patch 7.42

The first numbered major patch since 7.41 (March 24). Valve touched almost every core number: hero health, spell damage, the gold and XP curves, item effects on illusions, the neutral item system, the map and all 127 heroes.

Pip (hit-count) health

Heroes, Roshan, towers and fountains deal 1 full pip; illusions and creeps deal ¼ pip. Couriers, Psionic Traps, Zeus's Nimbus and Roshan's Banner are now "Fragile": melee heroes deal 2 pips to them.

Break rules

Some innates are fully disabled by Break (Morphling, Leshrac, Terrorblade, Io). Others get a "partial" Break: no new stacks are gained, but existing ones stay (Windranger Tailwind, Dazzle Weave, Clinkz, Spirit Breaker).

Debuff immunity & lifesteal

24 abilities that pierce debuff immunity no longer ignore its magic resistance (e.g. Pudge Dismember, Magnus Reverse Polarity). Spell Lifesteal from multiple copies of the same item now stacks: two Voodoo Mask = 30%.

03 New stat: Item Potency

Item Potency is a new attribute on hero attacks. It defaults to 100% and sets how strongly on-hit item effects are applied. When the same debuff comes from several sources, the highest Potency wins.

The biggest consequence: illusions can now apply most on-hit effects (at 20% strength by default). Orb of Blight, Orb of Frost, Orb of Corrosion, Desolator, Diffusal Blade, Disperser, Eye of Skadi, Mage Slayer, Monkey King Bar and Parasma are on the list. Skull Basher and a few others are excluded.

Valve's example: the Tier 4 "Evolved" enchantment gives +20% Potency, so Desolator removes 7.2 armor instead of 6.

Luna's Moon Glaives bounces are now full attacks (with reduced damage) and each bounce lowers Potency by 40%; lifesteal and crits don't apply to bounces.

04 Spell Power Reduction

Spell amp, AoE and cast range from items were reduced, and many spells deal less raw damage. Combined with base health rising to 200, one-combo kills are harder in the early game.

From the 2nd death onward it also drops an Aghanim's Shard every time (gold instead if the team already has one).

New: Greater Horn of Battle NEW

The Tormentor always drops it. Global range, no mana cost or cooldown, one charge, usable even from the backpack. Using it summons a mega creep wave in every lane (each with at least one siege creep and one Flagbearer).

Roshan & Glyph

  • Roshan's respawn timer gets 1 minute shorter after each death (minimum 5 minutes).
  • The timer now shows who is holding the Aegis of the Immortal.
  • Glyph of Fortification: no longer resets when buildings fall; 2 charges, 6-minute replenish, 7 s between uses.

Creep waves

  • The melee creep count no longer grows at 30:00; instead every wave after 30:00 has a Flagbearer.
  • The extra ranged creep arrives at 35:00 (was 40:00).
  • Siege: one every 2.5 minutes from 30:00, two from 60:00.
  • Lane creeps pulled out of lane slow by up to 25% over 10 s (pulls near the safelane T1 tower don't count).

Runes, Outposts

The Invisibility Rune grants a "Disguise" buff 2 s after pickup that protects against True Sight while away from enemy heroes and buildings (like Smoke without the speed bonus). Outposts can now be captured while silenced.

07 Map and neutral camps

What moved? (schematic, not to scale · old gate positions approximate)

Mid T1 towers moved ~350 units and T2 towers ~260 units away from the Radiant base (to even out distances). The Radiant mid wave now spawns in front of its T3, like Dire's.

Map changes

  • Twin Gates moved from the high ground near the stream ends to the top and bottom outer rim of the map (the flooded rim streams); one gate takes the old small flooded camp's spot.
  • Small flooded camps moved closer to the stream ends; medium camps moved onto the high ground the gates used to occupy.
  • The mid river was reshaped, with trees added to break lines of sight.
  • The Dire fountain moved ~300 units closer to the Dire Ancient; fountain True Sight radius 1,200 → 1,800.
  • Siege creeps no longer switch to towers that are attacking heroes.

Neutral camps: "home forest"

Every non-amphibian, non-ancient camp now belongs to one side and only spawns there. Neutral creeps can walk through both teams' defender gates.

10 Neutral item system

5 tiers → 4 tiers. Each tier has 4 pools of 3 artifacts, and you pick one from each pool. All options are fixed at the start of the match and shown in the Neutrals tab.

  • Tier 4 Madstone cost 10 → 14; each recraft +6 (was +10).
  • "Infinite Madstone" from 65:00.
  • Most enchantments are now Tier 1–3. Tier 4 "Evolved": +20 to primary attribute (was +40) and +20% Item Potency.
  • Revenant's Brooch is now a neutral item.
  • Red Ledger (new, T1): marks 2–5 enemy heroes sharing a trait; killing them grants bonus gold.

13 Client and performance updates

  • Individual hotkeys can be bound for Invoker's Invoked Spells, Largo's songs and Kez's Sai abilities.
  • Unit ability hotkeys added for Lycan's wolves and the new neutral creeps (Radigull, Odobenus Frostmage, Eimermole Foreman, Ancient Ogre Seal, etc.).
  • Option to show silhouettes for visually obstructed units.
  • Hand of Midas is now its own category in Dota+ gold breakdowns.
  • Better client performance when spawning illusions and server performance around hero deaths; the unit behavior state machine was refactored.
  • Bug fixes: the Meepo TP-selling crash, Naga Siren's Mirror Image killing unrelated illusions, the Oracle Prognostication HUD position.
